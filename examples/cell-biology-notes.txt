Osmosis
Osmosis is the diffusion of water across a selectively permeable membrane.
Water moves toward the side with the higher solute concentration.
A hypotonic solution has a lower solute concentration than the cell, so water enters the cell.
A hypertonic solution has a higher solute concentration than the cell, so water leaves the cell.
In a plant cell, water entering from a hypotonic solution presses the membrane against the cell wall. That pressure is turgor.

Mitosis
Mitosis produces two nuclei with the same chromosome number as the parent nucleus.
Prophase: chromosomes condense and the nuclear envelope breaks down.
Metaphase: chromosomes line up at the cell's equator.
Anaphase: sister chromatids separate and move toward opposite poles.
Telophase: nuclear envelopes form around each set of chromosomes.
Cytokinesis divides the cytoplasm and, in plant cells, builds a cell plate.
